# Rules and Settings

A game takes a certain amount of rounds which is specified by the host before the game starts. though during the game the host may add additional rounds

The Board is empty of tiles in the beginning and the host can decide which tiles are allowed. he can have them randomly distributed or place them manually


# Tiles

Image Tiles (1 Player steps on a tile everyone can play and gather points. the player on the tile gets 2 for a win the others get 1 for a win)

- easy
  multiple Choice 
	anime cover
	anime character
	anime fan art

- medium
  multiple choice
	manga panel
	soundtrack clip (audio only)
	opening clip (audio and video)

- hard
  Type answer and answer is revealed when time runs out
	anime cover
	anime character
	manga character
	anime fanart
	anime cosplay
	
	
Shop Tile (Trade in Score points for Items)

- Piggy bank (deposit score so it cant be used or stolen)
- crowbar [1 time use] (hit player on the same tile over the head and steal 1 point)
- 

Co-op Tiles (the players are put into 2 teams and compete, the winning teams members get 1 point, the player that steped onto the field has a 50/50 chance to be allowed to steal 1 point from any of the loosing members and award it to any player on the winning team himself included)

- truth or lie:
	the teams each get a random set of obscure quotes from anime wikis they can choose to modify it or not. the 	sets are switched after a set time and the players have to figure out whether or not they where modified
