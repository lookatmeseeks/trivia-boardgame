How to play
Roll a d6 and move one tile at a time. When you reach a junction, choose an available route to continue. The board contains loops, so routes can reconnect. Landing on ★ awards points. ! triggers a random score event. ? is reserved for future trivia challenges.
Prototype only · Trivia, online multiplayer, and saved game state are not implemented yet.
