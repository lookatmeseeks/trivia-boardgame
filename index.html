How to play
Roll a d6 and move one tile at a time. At junctions, choose a route. Image tiles let every player answer once: the player who landed there earns 2 points for a correct answer, and other players earn 1. Each question ends as soon as the first player answers correctly. Only that player earns points. Shop and co-op tile effects are not implemented yet.
